Pagelark

An on-device document reader and text-to-speech audio player for Android, iPhone and iPad. Listen to your documents, articles, books, and notes with synchronized word-level highlighting and background playback.

By NetSoftTouch · Android and iOS · App ID com.pagelark.reader

What it does

Multiple FormatsPDF, EPUB, DOCX, and TXT files
Web ArticlesFetch and read articles via URL or Share
Camera OCRScan printed pages with on-device text recognition
Pasted TextInstantly read or listen to text from your clipboard
Karaoke HighlightingWord and sentence-level sync during playback
Background AudioKeep listening with screen off or app minimized
Reading ToolsSleep timer, voice selection, speed & pitch controls

100% On-Device Privacy: Pagelark parses documents, recognizes camera text, and generates text-to-speech entirely on your local device. Your document contents and reading history are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in the cloud.

No Ads, No Tracking, and Pro

  • Pagelark has no ads, for everyone, on the free plan too.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no account, and no consent pop-ups. On iOS it collects no data; on Android only Google ML Kit’s technical diagnostics leave the device (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Everything works offline except fetching a web article you ask for and buying or restoring Pro.
  • The free plan keeps 5 documents in your library, scans 10 pages a day and includes 1 audio export a day (up to 20 minutes). It reads at up to 1.5× with the normal voice pitch; faster speeds (up to 3×), other pitches, Skip citations & links and shake to extend the sleep timer can each be tried 3 times for free.
  • An optional Pagelark Pro upgrade (monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play on Android or the App Store on iOS) removes all of these limits.
