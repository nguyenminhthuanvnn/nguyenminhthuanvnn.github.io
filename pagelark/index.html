Pagelark

An on-device document reader and text-to-speech audio player for Android. Listen to your documents, articles, books, and notes with synchronized word-level highlighting and background playback.

By NetSoftTouch · Package com.pagelark.reader

What it does

Multiple FormatsPDF, EPUB, DOCX, and TXT files
Web ArticlesFetch and read articles via URL or Share
Camera OCRScan printed pages with on-device text recognition
Pasted TextInstantly read or listen to text from your clipboard
Karaoke HighlightingWord and sentence-level sync during playback
Background AudioKeep listening with screen off or app minimized
Android AutoListen to your library from your car display
Reading ToolsSleep timer, voice selection, speed & pitch controls

100% On-Device Privacy: Pagelark parses documents, recognizes camera text, and generates text-to-speech entirely on your local device. Your document contents and reading history are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in the cloud.

Ads and Pro Upgrade

  • The free version of Pagelark is supported by advertising (Google AdMob).
  • Users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and relevant US states are presented with a consent dialog powered by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and can adjust their choices at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.
  • An optional Pagelark Pro upgrade is available via Google Play In-App Billing to remove ads and unlock unlimited reading.
  • Anonymous usage analytics and crash diagnostics help improve app reliability and can be toggled off at any time in Settings → Usage & crash reporting.
