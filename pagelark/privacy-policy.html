Privacy Policy

How Pagelark collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Application: Pagelark for Android and iOS (com.pagelark.reader) · Developer: NetSoftTouch (Nguyen Minh Thuan)

This policy covers Pagelark on both Android (Google Play) and iPhone and iPad (the App Store). The two apps work the same way. Where they differ, the text says so with On Android or On iOS. A short summary in Vietnamese is at the end.

Summary: Pagelark has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no user accounts. Your documents, scanned pages and audio exports are processed and stored only on your device and are never uploaded. On iOS, the app collects no data. On Android, the only data that leaves the device is the limited technical diagnostics Google ML Kit sends to Google (Section 2B); it never includes your documents, images or text. This policy describes Pagelark 1.3.0 and later; earlier versions showed ads and used Google AdMob and Firebase.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Document content and text: files you open or import (PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT), text you paste and pages you scan are parsed, stored and displayed only on your device.
  • Text-to-speech audio: speech is generated on your device by its built-in text-to-speech engine. On Android, this is the system engine (such as Google Text-to-Speech), and Pagelark only picks voices that the engine reports as working offline. On iOS, Pagelark uses Apple’s built-in voices, which run on the device. The text being read is never sent to a cloud speech service.
  • Accounts: Pagelark has no registration or login and never asks for your name, email address or password.
  • Your library, reading progress and searches: documents, bookmarks, reading positions, favourites and the words you search for stay in the app’s private database on your device.
  • Usage and advertising data: Pagelark contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not read your device’s advertising identifier, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not send usage statistics to us. The only exception is the ML Kit diagnostics on Android described in Section 2B.

2. Services the App Relies On

A. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing and the App Store)

If you buy or subscribe to Pagelark Pro, the transaction is processed by the store you installed the app from. On Android, that is Google Play Billing, under the Google Play Terms of Service. On iOS, purchases are made through the App Store: Apple processes the payment under its own terms, and the app receives only whether you hold Pro. NetSoftTouch does not receive, store or have access to your card, bank, Apple Account or Google account details.

B. On-Device Text Recognition and Language Detection

On Android, Camera OCR reads pages with Google ML Kit’s text-recognition models and, for languages ML Kit does not cover, with the open-source Tesseract OCR engine and its public tessdata_fast language models. All of these models are bundled inside the app: scanning, in any language, downloads nothing, and the image and the recognised text are never sent to Google or anyone else. To pick a voice for a document that does not say what language it is in, Pagelark uses Google ML Kit’s Language Identification, which is also bundled and runs on your device.

Like other Google SDKs, ML Kit sends Google limited technical information to monitor and improve the SDK — device model and OS version, app package name and version, a per-installation identifier, which ML Kit feature was used, and performance and error metrics. It never includes your images or text, is not used for advertising, and there is no setting to turn it off. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

On iOS, Pagelark does not use ML Kit. Pages are read with Apple’s Vision framework, and Tesseract with the same bundled tessdata_fast models handles languages Vision does not cover. A document’s language is detected with Apple’s Natural Language framework. All of this runs on your device; no image or text is sent anywhere.

C. Crash Reports You Choose to Send

On iOS, when the app crashes or freezes, iOS may give Pagelark a technical report (through Apple’s MetricKit). Pagelark keeps it on your device. It contains stack traces and device details such as the model and iOS version, never your documents, titles or file names. Nothing is sent automatically: if you choose Settings → Send diagnostic report, your Mail app opens with the reports attached and you decide whether to send the email. Reports you send are used only to fix the problem and are deleted from your device once sent.

Separately, if you have turned on Share iPhone Analytics / Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash logs with developers through Apple’s own systems. That is controlled by Apple’s settings and privacy policy, not by Pagelark.

3. Network Use

Pagelark works offline. It connects to the internet only for:

  • Web articles you ask for: when you share or paste a web address, the app downloads that page directly from its website over HTTPS to extract the article text. No personal identifiers or other documents are sent. As with any web request, that website receives your IP address and standard request information, and its own privacy policy applies.
  • Purchases: buying, restoring and checking Pagelark Pro, through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS).
  • Links you open: the privacy policy, terms of use and store pages open in your browser or store app.
  • ML Kit diagnostics (Android only): the technical information described in Section 2B.

Camera OCR, reading aloud and audio export never use the network.

Information shown to other parts of your device

These stay on your device and your own connected accessories, and are not sent to us:

  • Playback controls: while a document is playing, its title is shown in the notification, on the lock screen, and to devices and apps you connect for media control, such as Bluetooth headsets, Android Auto, Wear OS or Google Assistant (on Android), or Control Center, AirPods and Apple Watch (on iOS) — the same as for any audio app.
  • Audio exports: an exported MP3 or M4A file is named after the document and is never uploaded. On Android, it is saved to your device’s shared Music/Pagelark folder (on Android 9 and older, to Pagelark’s own Music folder), so music players and file managers on your device can open it. On iOS, it is saved in an Exports folder inside Pagelark’s own storage, which you can open in the Files app (On My iPhone → Pagelark) or through Finder on a connected Mac.
  • Shared links (iOS): when you share a web link to Pagelark from another app, the share extension passes only that link to the app, on your device.

4. Device Permissions and Why They Are Needed

Pagelark requests only the permissions its features need.

On iOS

  • Camera: asked for only when you first scan a page. The photo is read on your device (Section 2B) and deleted straight after. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • Add to Photos: asked for only when you save a quote card image. Pagelark can add images but cannot see your photo library.
  • Photos and Files: pictures and documents are chosen with Apple’s system pickers. Pagelark receives only the items you pick, so iOS does not ask for a permission.
  • Background audio: lets reading continue with the screen locked or while you use another app. It is not a permission prompt.
  • Pagelark never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track you.

On Android

  • android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: fetching web articles you ask for, verifying Pro purchases, and the ML Kit diagnostics in Section 2B.
  • android.permission.CAMERA: only when you use Camera OCR. A captured page is kept temporarily in the app’s private cache while its text is read on the device, then deleted. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS and android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: media controls in the notification drawer and on the lock screen during background playback.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING: lets an audio export you started finish, with a progress notification, if you leave the app.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE: required by Android for the two foreground services above.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: keeps the device awake while audio is playing.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: a short vibration to confirm that shaking the phone extended the sleep timer.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: required by Google Play Billing to sell and verify Pagelark Pro.
  • Storage / document access: Pagelark uses the Android system file picker, so you choose specific files without granting access to your whole storage.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Pagelark is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at nguyenminhthuanvnn@gmail.com so we can take immediate corrective action.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your documents, their text, your reading progress, bookmarks, search history and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them, so we hold no copy to delete:

  • You can delete any document from the Pagelark library at any time within the app.
  • On Android, clearing the app’s data in Android Settings or uninstalling Pagelark permanently removes all locally stored documents, reading progress and settings. Exported audio files in Music/Pagelark are ordinary files on your device and stay until you delete them.
  • On iOS, deleting Pagelark from your iPhone or iPad removes everything it stored, including your library, reading progress, settings, kept crash reports and the audio files in its Exports folder. Move any export you want to keep to another folder in the Files app first.
  • Device backups: like most apps, Pagelark’s storage may be included when you back up your device (iCloud or a computer on iOS). Those backups are made by Apple or Google and you, are covered by their privacy policies, and are never sent to us. On Android, your documents and library are excluded from Android cloud backup.
  • Emails you send us (feedback or a diagnostic report) are kept in our mailbox only as long as needed to answer you and fix the problem. Ask us at any time to delete them.

How Data Is Protected

  • Everything the app sends over the network travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS); the app does not allow unencrypted traffic.
  • Your documents and library are kept in the app’s private storage, which the operating system prevents other apps from reading.
  • We never receive your documents, so there is no copy of them on any server of ours to be exposed.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Pagelark does not collect, sell or share personal information: your documents and reading data exist only on your device, where you can view and delete them directly. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to access or delete that correspondence. You are welcome to contact us with any privacy question.
