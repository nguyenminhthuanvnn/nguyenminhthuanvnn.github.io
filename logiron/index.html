LogIron

A gym workout log and training planner for Android and Wear OS. Plan your week, log every set, rest on time and see your progress. Your training history stays on your device, and no account is required.

By NetSoftTouch · Package com.logiron.app

What it does

Weekly plannerLay out a training week and reuse sessions across days
Fast set loggingWeight × reps steppers, warm-up sets, effort (RIR / RPE)
Rest timerKeeps counting with the screen off, with sound and vibration
Supersets & giant setsLink exercises and train them in rounds
Exercise guidesAnimated form guides and an optional voice coach
ProgressPersonal records, volume and body measurements
Health ConnectRead steps, sleep and weight; save workouts
Wear OS & heart rateLog from your watch or a Bluetooth heart rate strap

On-device first: your routines, workouts, sets, body measurements and notes are stored in a private database on your phone. LogIron has no account system and no server of its own, and it never uploads your training or health data.

Ads and the "Remove ads" purchase

  • Every feature of LogIron is free. The app is supported by advertising served by Google AdMob.
  • A one-time Remove ads purchase through Google Play turns all advertising off. Nothing else is locked behind it.
  • Users in regions that require consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states) are asked through the Google User Messaging Platform, and can change their choice at any time under Settings → Privacy → Privacy options.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports can be turned off under Settings → Privacy → Share usage & crash reports.

Health notice

LogIron is a training log, not a medical device, and it does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise programme, especially if you have a health condition, and stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
