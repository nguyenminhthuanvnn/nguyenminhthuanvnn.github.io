Privacy Policy

How LogIron handles your training, health and device information.

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Application: LogIron (com.logiron.app) and LogIron for Wear OS · Developer: NetSoftTouch

Summary: LogIron stores your workouts, routines, body measurements and notes only on your own devices. There is no account and no LogIron server. Health Connect and heart rate data are used only to show and record your training, are never sent to us, and are never used for advertising. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob and sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase — only after you opt in if you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, and until you opt out elsewhere.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is created by you and stored in the app's private database on your phone (and, if you use it, on your paired Wear OS watch). We never receive it.

  • Training data: routines, weekly plans, workouts, sets, weights, repetitions, effort ratings (RIR / RPE), rest times, personal records and notes.
  • Body data: body weight, body-fat percentage and measurements (chest, arm, waist) you enter, and your maximum heart rate setting.
  • Heart rate during workouts: readings from a Bluetooth heart rate strap or from the LogIron watch app, saved with the workout they belong to.
  • Preferences: units, rest timer, language, theme, reminders and the answers you give during onboarding (training goal, experience level, days per week).

The routine generator, exercise form guides and the voice coach all run on your device. The voice coach uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Android backup

LogIron does not take part in Android's cloud backup to your Google account, because its database can contain data read from Health Connect. When you move to a new phone with Android's direct device-to-device transfer (cable or Wi-Fi between the two phones), Android copies LogIron's database and settings straight to the new phone; nothing passes through us. To keep a copy elsewhere, use Settings → Data → Create backup, which saves a file to a location you choose.

2. Health Connect

If you connect LogIron to Health Connect, and only for the permissions you grant, LogIron:

  • Reads steps, sleep and body weight, to show them next to your training on the Body screen and, when you ask, to import your weight history.
  • Writes your finished workouts as exercise sessions, together with their heart rate and active calories, so other health apps can see your training.

Data read from Health Connect is shown in the app and, where you import it, stored in LogIron's on-device database. It is not sent to NetSoftTouch or any third party, is not used for advertising or analytics, and is not sold. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect's settings; data LogIron already wrote to Health Connect stays there until you delete it in Health Connect.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Services that collect information

A. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, native, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about ad views and clicks. Ad requests never contain your training or health data.

Consent: in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, LogIron uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your choice before ads are requested. You can change it at any time under Settings → Privacy → Privacy options. If you do not consent to personalised advertising, only limited or non-personalised ads are requested.

Remove ads: after the one-time Remove ads purchase, LogIron makes no ad requests at all.

B. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

LogIron sends anonymous usage statistics: which screens are opened, which features are used (for example "workout finished" with counts and rounded durations), app version, device model and operating system, and a few settings-level properties such as training goal, experience level, days per week, whether ads are removed and whether a program is active. It never includes weights, training volume, heart rate, body data, Health Connect data or any name you type.

Consent before collection, where consent is required. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, usage statistics and crash reports are switched off and collect nothing unless and until you turn on Settings → Privacy → Share usage & crash reports. Answering the advertising consent form does not switch them on — advertising and analytics are separate purposes, and that form covers only advertising. Elsewhere they start enabled and you can turn them off at any time. In every region both are held off when the app starts until your region has been determined, so nothing is collected before that point.

C. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Crashlytics collects the stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and disk, and the name of the screen that was open. Crash reports contain no training, body or health data.

Crash reporting follows the same single choice as usage statistics, described above.

D. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase is active.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Sharing you start yourself

  • Routine and program links / QR codes: when you share a routine or a weekly plan, its exercises, sets and reps are encoded into the link or QR code itself. It goes only where you send it; no server is involved.
  • Workout summary cards: an image you choose to share through Android's share sheet.
  • Backup, restore and CSV export / import: files are written to and read from a location you pick with Android's file picker. LogIron does not upload them anywhere.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: ads, analytics, crash reports and purchase verification.
  • Notifications: the rest timer while the screen is off, and the workout reminders you schedule.
  • Foreground service: keeps the rest timer counting, with its skip and add-time controls in the notification, while the screen is off or the app is in the background.
  • Vibration: rest timer alerts.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): only to find and connect a heart rate strap you choose. LogIron does not use Bluetooth to determine your location. On Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission for any Bluetooth scan; LogIron requests it only for that purpose and does not read your location.
  • Health Connect permissions: described in section 2.
  • Body sensors / heart rate (watch app): the Wear OS app reads heart rate during a workout you start on the watch.
  • Camera: LogIron does not request the camera permission. QR codes are scanned by Google Play services' code scanner, which returns only the scanned text to the app.

6. Wear OS

The phone and watch apps exchange the current workout, rest timer, set confirmations and heart rate directly through Google Play services' Wearable Data Layer. This data travels only between your own paired devices.

7. Children

LogIron is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can delete routines, programs and sets in the app. Uninstalling LogIron, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes everything LogIron stored on the device.
  • Data LogIron wrote to Health Connect is managed and deleted in Health Connect.
  • Firebase keeps analytics data for a limited period (at most 14 months) and crash reports for up to 90 days. Because this data is not linked to your name or an account, email us from the app (Settings → Send feedback) with your request and we will help using the identifiers available.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA / CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Most of these are available directly in the app through the controls above; for anything else, contact us. We do not sell personal information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
