Privacy Policy

How Stillscribe for Android handles your recordings, transcripts and other information.

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Application: Stillscribe for Android · Developer: NetSoftTouch (Minh Thuan Nguyen)

This Privacy Policy explains how Stillscribe for Android (“the App”), provided by NetSoftTouch (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you use it, including its notification controls, Quick Settings tile and home screen widget. Stillscribe for iPhone has its own Privacy Policy.

Summary: Stillscribe records, transcribes, labels speakers, summarizes and translates entirely on your phone. It has no user accounts and no servers of ours, and contains no advertising and no analytics of our own. Your audio, transcripts, summaries and notes are stored only on your phone, and we never receive them. Google libraries that the App uses send Google technical data about how they run (Section 5); this never includes your recordings or text.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect any information from the App. In particular:

  • Audio recordings: recordings you make, and audio or video files you import, share into the App or place in a watched folder, are saved in the App’s private storage on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • Transcripts and speaker labels: speech is turned into text on the phone by speech models that run inside the App (and, for live English text on Android 12 or later, by Google’s on-device ML Kit speech recognition). The audio is not sent to a cloud speech service.
  • AI summaries: summaries, key points, decisions and action items are generated on the phone, either by Gemini Nano through Android’s AICore system service, or by an AI model downloaded to the phone. Your transcript is not sent to us, to Google’s servers or to any AI provider for this.
  • Translation: translation runs on the phone with Google’s ML Kit translation models. Your text is not sent anywhere to be translated.
  • Notes and settings: titles, highlights, speaker names, custom words, summary templates, search terms and settings stay on your phone.
  • Accounts: the App has no sign-up or login and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • Advertising and tracking: the App contains no advertising software, does not use the advertising ID, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Location, contacts and photos: the App does not access them.

2. Data stored on your phone

To work, the App keeps the following in its private storage, which Android protects from other apps:

DataPurpose
Audio filesThe original recording, kept so you can listen again. Recordings are written to a file first so a recording is never lost.
Transcripts, summaries, translationsTo show, search and export your notes.
Templates, highlights, speaker names, custom words, settingsTo remember your choices.
Free summary countTo count the free plan’s 3 summaries every 7 days. It is a count and dates only.
Speech, AI and translation modelsDownloaded once so that everything can run on the phone. They contain no information about you.
Crash reportsTechnical details of recent crashes (stack traces, app and Android version, phone model). They contain no audio or text, stay on the phone, and are sent only if you choose to, as described in Section 4.

We have no access to any of this data. It stays on the phone until you delete it. The App does not take part in Android backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account.

3. Permissions

The App asks for permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can change them at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Stillscribe → Permissions.

  • Microphone: to record audio. When you start a recording, the App keeps recording in the background (for example with the screen off) until you stop it, and Android shows that the microphone is in use and a notification for the recording.
  • Notifications (optional): to show recording, transcription and summary progress, to tell you when a summary is ready, and, if you start a free trial of Pro Yearly, one local reminder a day before the trial ends. All notifications are created on your phone.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock (optional): only if you turn on the App lock. Android handles the check; the App only receives a yes or no answer and never has access to your biometric data.
  • Folders (optional): when you choose a watched folder or an export folder, you pick it in Android’s folder picker, and the App can access only that folder. Files you import or share come through Android’s file picker or Share menu; the App only receives the files you pick or share.

Sending action items to your calendar opens your calendar app with the event filled in; the App does not read your calendar.

4. When information leaves your phone

The App never sends your recordings, transcripts, summaries or notes anywhere by itself. Information leaves your phone only in these cases:

  • Exporting and sharing: when you export or share a note (PDF, Markdown, plain text or SRT), or send action items to another app, it goes to the app or person you choose. What happens next is governed by that app or service.
  • Automatic export to a folder (Pro, optional): if you turn it on, the App saves Markdown files to the folder you picked. If another app syncs that folder (for example a cloud drive or a notes app), that app may upload them under its own terms.
  • Diagnostic reports: if you tap Send diagnostic report in Settings, the App shows you the full report first and then opens Android’s Share menu so you can choose how to send it. It contains technical details only, never recordings or text. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.
  • Emails to us: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you.
  • Google technical data: described in Section 5.

5. Google services

The App uses services and libraries from Google. When they contact Google, they are operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us:

  • Google Play purchases: purchases and subscriptions of Stillscribe Pro are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details, name or Google account. The App receives only the purchase status from Google Play Billing so it can unlock Pro.
  • Model downloads: speech and AI models are downloaded from Google Play (Play Asset Delivery) the first time they are needed, and translation and speech-recognition language packs from Google through ML Kit. Gemini Nano is downloaded and managed by Android’s AICore. These downloads only fetch models; they do not contain your recordings or text.
  • ML Kit technical data: the App uses Google’s ML Kit for live English text, language detection, translation and Gemini Nano summaries. ML Kit processes your audio and text on the phone and does not send them or its results to Google. Google states that ML Kit sends Google technical data so it can debug and improve ML Kit: device information (manufacturer, model, Android version, available ML hardware), app information (package name and version), a per-installation identifier, performance metrics such as latency, and error codes. This data is encrypted in transit and is not linked to your name or account by us. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy. On Google Play, the App’s Data safety section lists this data.
  • Crash data from Google Play: if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your phone, Google may share crash and performance data about the App with developers through Google Play Console, under Google’s own terms.

6. Recording other people

Stillscribe is a tool for recording conversations you are part of or have the right to record. Laws in many places, including several U.S. states, require the consent of everyone being recorded. You are responsible for telling people and getting any consent the law requires. See the Terms of Use.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • A recording: delete it in the App. Its audio file, transcript, summary and translations are removed from your phone. Files the App already exported or shared are not affected.
  • Downloaded models: remove them in Settings → Speech models; they download again when needed.
  • Everything: uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes all of its data from your phone.
  • Emails and diagnostic reports you sent us: email us and we will delete them.

Because we do not hold your recordings or any account, and the App’s data is not included in Android backups, we cannot restore deleted recordings or recordings lost with a phone. Please export anything you want to keep.

8. Security

Your data is stored in the App’s private storage, which Android isolates from other apps and encrypts on phones with file-based encryption (standard on Android 11 and later). You can add an App lock in Settings, which also hides the App’s content in the recent apps screen. Because your content never reaches a server of ours, there is no copy of it that could be exposed in a breach on our side.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Since we do not collect personal information through the App, your data is on your phone, where you can view, export and delete it at any time. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. For data Google processes (Section 5), Google’s privacy controls apply. For any information you sent us by email, contact us to exercise these rights, and we will respond within the time the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If a change would let data leave your phone in a new way, we will explain it in the App before it takes effect.
