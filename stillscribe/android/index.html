Stillscribe for Android

Private AI voice notes

Record meetings, lectures, interviews and voice memos. Stillscribe turns them into searchable transcripts and clear summaries, all on your phone. Transcription is free and unlimited in 10 languages, with no minute caps, no account and no upload.

By NetSoftTouch · Android 11 or later, 64-bit phones · Coming to Google Play

What it does

Record anywhereKeeps recording with the screen off or in other apps; pause or stop from the notification
Never lose a recordingAudio is saved to a file as you record. If a call comes in, recording pauses and resumes in the same file
Unlimited and freeNo minute caps on recording or transcription, even on the free plan
10 languagesEnglish, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian and Ukrainian. Pick one before you record
Live textSee the words on screen as you speak
AI summariesOverview, key points, decisions and action items, written by Gemini Nano on the phone
TemplatesMeeting, Lecture, Interview and Voice memo, or your own
AskAsk a question about a recording; answers link to the moment it was said
Tap to listenTap any line of the transcript to hear that moment; star highlights while recording
Search everythingSearch all transcripts and open the matching line
ImportImport audio or video files, or share them to Stillscribe from other apps
Share cardsShare a summary, or any transcript line, as an image card
Calendar and tasksSend action items to your calendar or a task app, with due dates filled in
ExportPlain text, or PDF, Markdown and SRT subtitles with Pro
App lockKeep your recordings behind your fingerprint, face or screen lock

Your recordings never leave your phone. Recording, transcription, summaries and answers all run on the device. Stillscribe has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics of its own, and we never receive your audio, transcripts or summaries. The app downloads speech models from Google Play; Google libraries in the app send Google anonymous technical data about how they run, never your content. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Free and Pro

FreeUnlimited recording and transcription in every language, live text, search, highlights, import, share cards, 3 AI summaries every 7 days, 2 questions per recording with Ask, plain-text export.
ProUnlimited AI summaries and questions, your own summary templates, calendar and tasks, PDF/Markdown/SRT export, search filters, the app lock, and no Stillscribe credit on exports and cards.
  • Pro Monthly: billed every month, renews automatically.
  • Pro Yearly: billed every year, renews automatically. Eligible new subscribers may get a free trial, and the app reminds you a day before it ends.
  • Pro Lifetime: one-time purchase, no subscription.

Google Play shows the price in your currency, and any free trial, before you buy. Payment is charged to your Google account. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Full details in the Terms of Use.

Requirements

  • A 64-bit Android phone with Android 11 or later.
  • English works right away: Google Play downloads the English speech model (about 140 MB) once, right after you install. The other nine languages use a larger model (about 670 MB) that downloads once from Google Play the first time you pick one; Wi-Fi is recommended. After that, recording and transcription work offline. Recordings made before a download finishes are transcribed once it does.
  • AI summaries and Ask use Gemini Nano, Google’s on-device AI model, which runs only on supported phones, such as recent Pixel phones. The app checks your phone and tells you. Without Gemini Nano, recording, transcription, search and export all still work.
  • Stillscribe can’t record phone calls. Android doesn’t let apps do that.

Open-source models

  • NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (English and other European languages) and NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 110M (English), © NVIDIA, CC BY 4.0, converted to ONNX and quantized by the sherpa-onnx project.
  • sherpa-onnx, Silero VAD and ONNX Runtime.

Full license texts are in the app: Settings → About → Open-source licenses.

Support
