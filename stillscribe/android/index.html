Stillscribe for Android

Private AI voice notes

Record meetings, lectures, interviews and voice memos. Stillscribe turns them into searchable transcripts and clear summaries, all on your phone. Transcription is free and unlimited in 18 languages, with no minute caps, no account and no upload.

By NetSoftTouch · Android 11 or later, 64-bit phones · Coming to Google Play

What it does

Record anywhereKeeps recording with the screen off or in other apps; pause or stop from the notification, a Quick Settings tile or the widget
Never lose a recordingAudio is saved to a file as you record. If the phone or app stops, the recording is recovered
Unlimited and freeNo minute caps on recording or transcription, even on the free plan
18 languagesPick one or let the app detect it, including recordings that mix two languages
Who said whatMeetings are split by speaker; rename Speaker 1 to a real name
AI summariesOverview, key points, decisions and action items, written by an AI model on the phone
TemplatesMeeting, Lecture, Interview and Voice memo, or your own
Tap to listenTap any line of the transcript to hear that moment; star highlights while recording
Search everythingSearch all transcripts and summaries and open the matching line
Import and watched foldersShare audio or video from any app, or let a folder (call recordings, a voice recorder) import new files by itself
Calendar and tasksSend action items to your calendar or a task app, with due dates filled in
ExportPDF, Markdown, plain text or SRT subtitles, or save Markdown to a folder automatically (for example an Obsidian vault)
Offline translationTranslate a transcript and its summary on the phone
App lockKeep your recordings behind your fingerprint, face or screen lock

Your recordings never leave your phone. Recording, transcription, speaker labels, summaries and translation all run on the device. Stillscribe has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics of its own, and we never receive your audio, transcripts or summaries. The app downloads speech and AI models from Google Play; Google libraries in the app send Google anonymous technical data about how they run, never your content. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Free and Pro

FreeUnlimited recording and transcription in every language, speaker labels, search, highlights, import and watched folders, 3 AI summaries every 7 days, plain-text export.
ProUnlimited AI summaries, your own summary templates, calendar and tasks, PDF/Markdown/SRT export, automatic export to a folder, offline translation, search filters and the app lock.
  • Pro Monthly: billed every month, renews automatically.
  • Pro Yearly: billed every year, renews automatically. Eligible new subscribers may get a free trial, and the app reminds you a day before it ends.
  • Pro Lifetime: one-time purchase, no subscription.

Google Play shows the price in your currency, and any free trial, before you buy. Payment is charged to your Google account. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Full details in the Terms of Use.

Requirements

  • A 64-bit Android phone with Android 11 or later.
  • Speech models download from Google Play the first time a language needs one (for English about 460 MB, for Vietnamese about 140 MB). Large downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless you choose mobile data. After that, recording and transcription work offline. Recordings made before the download are transcribed once it finishes.
  • AI summaries use Gemini Nano on phones that offer it. Other phones with enough memory can download an AI model (about 1 GB, only when you choose to); on slower phones, long summaries finish while the phone is charging. On phones with less memory, summaries are not available, and the app says so. Recording, transcription, search and export work on every supported phone.

Support
