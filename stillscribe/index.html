Stillscribe

Private AI voice notes

Record meetings, lectures, interviews and voice memos. Stillscribe turns them into searchable transcripts and clear summaries, all on your iPhone. Transcription is free and unlimited, with no minute caps, no account and no upload.

By NetSoftTouch · iPhone, iOS 26 or later · English speech

What it does

Record anywhereKeeps recording with the screen locked; stop or pause from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
Live transcriptWords appear as people speak, and every recording is transcribed on the iPhone
Unlimited and freeNo minute caps on recording or transcription, even on the free plan
AI summariesOverview, key points, decisions and action items, written by Apple Intelligence on the device
TemplatesMeeting, Lecture, Interview and Voice memo, or your own
HighlightsStar the moment while recording, then jump straight back to it
Tap to listenTap any line of the transcript to hear that moment
Search everythingSearch all transcripts and open the matching line
Import and shareBring in audio and video from Voice Memos, Zoom, WhatsApp or Files
RemindersSend action items to Reminders with due dates filled in
ExportPDF, Markdown, plain text or SRT subtitles
Face ID lockKeep your recordings behind Face ID

Nothing leaves your iPhone. Recording, transcription and summaries all run on the device, using Apple’s on-device speech recognition and Apple Intelligence. Stillscribe has no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads. We never receive your audio, transcripts or summaries. See the Privacy Policy.

Free and Pro

FreeUnlimited recording and transcription, search, highlights, import, 3 AI summaries every 7 days, plain-text export.
ProUnlimited AI summaries, your own summary templates, Reminders, PDF/Markdown/SRT export, search filters and Face ID lock.
  • Pro Monthly: billed every month, renews automatically.
  • Pro Yearly: billed every year, renews automatically. Eligible new subscribers may get a free trial, and the app reminds you a day before it ends.
  • Pro Lifetime: one-time purchase, no subscription.

The App Store shows the price in your currency, and any free trial, before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Full details in the Terms of Use.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Transcription is in English.
  • A one-time internet connection is needed to download Apple’s speech model. After that, recording and transcription work offline. Recordings made before the download are transcribed once you are back online.
  • AI summaries need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. The app shows you how to turn it on. Recording, transcription, search and export work on every supported iPhone.

Support
