Privacy Policy

How PhotoPDF handles your photos and documents.

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Application: PhotoPDF for iPhone and iPad (com.photopdf.app) · Developer: NetSoftTouch

This policy covers PhotoPDF on iPhone and iPad (the App Store). A short summary in Vietnamese is at the end.

Summary: PhotoPDF does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it does not connect to the internet. Photos and PDFs you choose are processed only on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Photos and documents: the photos you pick and the PDFs you open are read, resized, compressed and turned into new PDFs on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Accounts: PhotoPDF has no registration or login and never asks for your name, email address or any other contact details.
  • Usage data, identifiers and location: PhotoPDF contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. It does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not ask to track you and does not use your location.

2. Network Access

PhotoPDF does not make network requests. It works the same in Airplane Mode.

3. Access to Your Photos and Files

  • Photos: photos are chosen with Apple’s system photo picker. PhotoPDF receives only the photos you select and cannot see the rest of your library, so iOS does not ask for a Photos permission.
  • Files: PDFs are chosen with Apple’s system document picker. PhotoPDF receives only the file you select. Your original file is never changed.
  • Sharing and saving: a PDF you create leaves the app only when you share it or save it with the iOS share sheet, and it goes only where you send it.

4. Storage and Deletion

While you work, copies of the photos and PDFs you chose and the PDFs you create are kept in the app’s temporary folder on your device. The app deletes this folder every time it starts, and iOS may also clear it when storage is low. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

5. Children

PhotoPDF is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of PhotoPDF changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

7. Contact
