Privacy Policy

How DualConvo handles your voice, your words and their translations.

Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Application: DualConvo: Voice Translator (Android) · Developer: NetSoftTouch (Minh Thuan Nguyen)

This Privacy Policy explains how DualConvo: Voice Translator (“the App”), provided by NetSoftTouch (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you use it. The App is a face-to-face voice translator: two people take turns speaking, and the phone translates and speaks each turn in the other person’s language.

Summary: DualConvo recognises speech, translates and speaks entirely on your phone. It has no user accounts and no servers of ours, and contains no advertising and no analytics of our own. Your voice, what you say and its translations are never uploaded and we never receive them. A Google library the App uses for translation sends Google technical data about how it runs (Section 5); this never includes your voice or text.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect any information from the App. In particular:

  • Your voice: the App listens only after you tap the microphone button, or while hands-free mode is on during a conversation. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s on-device speech recognizer (on most phones Google’s “Speech Recognition & Synthesis” service, running in offline mode). The App does not record, save or upload audio.
  • What you say and its translation: translation runs on the phone with Google’s ML Kit translation models. Your text is not sent anywhere to be translated.
  • Spoken translations: translations are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone.
  • Accounts: the App has no sign-up or login and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • Advertising and tracking: the App contains no advertising software, does not use the advertising ID, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Location, contacts and photos: the App does not access them.

2. Data on your phone

DataPurpose
Current conversationSo both people can read the last turn and scroll back through the conversation. It is kept in memory only, and is gone when you tap Clear or the App is closed. It is never written to storage.
SettingsYour two chosen languages and the hands-free switch, so the App remembers them.
Language modelsTranslation models (about 30 MB per language), speech-recognition models and voices, downloaded once so everything can run offline. They contain no information about you.

We have no access to any of this data. The App does not take part in Android backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account.

3. Permissions

You can change permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → DualConvo → Permissions.

  • Microphone: to hear what each person says. The App asks the first time you tap the microphone button. Without it, the App cannot listen. The App does not listen in the background: it stops when you leave the conversation screen.
  • Internet and network state: to download language models, and to show whether you are online or offline.

4. When information leaves your phone

The App never sends your voice, what you say or its translations anywhere by itself. Information leaves your phone only in these cases:

  • Sharing: if you tap Share in the conversation history, the text goes to the app or person you choose. What happens next is governed by that app or service.
  • Emails to us: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you.
  • Google technical data: described in Section 5.

5. Google services

The App uses services and libraries from Google. When they contact Google, they are operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us:

  • Model downloads: translation models are downloaded from Google through ML Kit the first time you use a language. Speech-recognition models and voices are downloaded by your phone’s speech and text-to-speech services when you install them. These downloads only fetch models; they do not contain your voice or text.
  • ML Kit technical data: ML Kit processes your text on the phone and does not send it or its translations to Google. Google states that ML Kit sends Google technical data so it can debug and improve ML Kit: device information (manufacturer, model, Android version, available ML hardware), app information (package name and version), a per-installation identifier that does not identify you or your device, performance metrics such as latency, error codes, the source and target languages you configured, and the size of the text processed (not the text itself). This data is encrypted in transit and is not passed to third parties. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy and ML Kit’s data disclosure. On Google Play, the App’s Data safety section lists this data.
  • Speech recognition and voices: these are provided by separate apps on your phone (such as Google’s Speech Recognition & Synthesis), under their own terms. The App asks them to work in offline mode.
  • Crash data from Google Play: if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google on your phone, Google may share crash and performance data about the App with developers through Google Play Console, under Google’s own terms.

6. Translations

Translations are produced automatically by Google Translate technology (ML Kit) and may be wrong or incomplete. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, safety or other important decisions.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • A conversation: tap Clear in the conversation history, or close the App.
  • Everything: uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes its settings and translation models from your phone.
  • Emails you sent us: email us and we will delete them.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not collect personal information through the App, and we do not sell or share personal information or use it for targeted advertising. For data Google processes (Section 5), Google’s privacy controls apply. For any information you sent us by email, contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If a change would let data leave your phone in a new way, we will explain it in the App before it takes effect.
