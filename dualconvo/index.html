DualConvo: Voice Translator

By NetSoftTouch · Android · Package com.dualconvo.translator

A two-way voice translator for talking face to face. Put the phone on the table between you: each person speaks in their own language and reads the translation on their half of the screen. After a one-time language download, it works offline.

What it does

Face-to-face screenThe top half is turned around for the person opposite you
Hands-freeListens to each side in turn, translates and speaks the result
Works offlineSpeech, translation and voices run on your phone, even in airplane mode
16 languagesDownload each language once, about 30 MB for translation

Private by design: your voice, what you say and its translations stay on your phone. DualConvo has no account, no ads and no server of its own.

Translation

  • Translation within DualConvo is powered by Google Translate, running on your phone through Google ML Kit. Learn more about Google Cloud Translation.
  • Translations are automatic and may be wrong. Do not rely on them for medical, legal or safety decisions.
  • Speech recognition uses your phone's on-device recognizer (on most phones Google's "Speech Recognition & Synthesis"). Requires Android 12 or later.
