NeoVid Converter

A video and audio converter for iPhone and iPad that does all its work on your device. Your files are never uploaded, there are no ads, and no account is needed.

By NetSoftTouch · iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 17 or later) · App ID com.neovid.converter · Looking for the Android app? See NeoVid Converter for Android.

What it does

Convert videoMP4, MOV, MKV, WebM and more, with HEVC on supported devices
Compress videoSmaller files at a resolution and quality you choose
Cut & mergeTrim clips, or join several videos or songs into one
Change speedSpeed up or slow down video and audio
Video to GIFTurn a clip into an animated GIF
Video to audioSave the soundtrack of a video as MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC and more
Audio toolsConvert, cut, merge and mix audio, and make ringtones
Share & open inSend files from Photos, Files or another app straight to a tool

Data Not Collected: NeoVid Converter has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting service. Conversions run on your iPhone or iPad with the FFmpeg libraries built into the app. The app connects to the internet only to buy or restore Pro through the App Store, and to open links you tap.

Free use and Pro

  • Every tool is free to use. The free plan includes 3 conversions on the day you start, then 1 conversion a day, reset at midnight. Only conversions that finish count.
  • Some options are Pro: hardware-accelerated encoding, 320 kbps audio, FLAC in the Audio Converter, Ultra compression, advanced video settings (bitrate, frame rate, HEVC), 1440p and 4K output, GIFs above 480p or 15 FPS, speeds outside 0.5×–2×, and merging or mixing more than 3 files. Each can be tried 3 times for free; a try is used only when the conversion finishes.
  • NeoVid Pro (a monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, through the App Store) removes the daily limit and unlocks all of these options. Prices are shown by the App Store in the app before you buy.

Support

  • Email nguyenminhthuanvnn@gmail.com with questions, feature suggestions or bug reports, or use Settings → Feedback in the app.
  • Please include your device model, its iOS or iPadOS version and the app version shown in Settings.
  • If a conversion fails, the bug button in its conversion log opens an email with the technical details, with file names and paths removed. After a crash or freeze, Settings → Send diagnostic report attaches the report iOS kept for the app.
  • To cancel a subscription, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. To get Pro back on a new device, use Restore purchases in the app.
