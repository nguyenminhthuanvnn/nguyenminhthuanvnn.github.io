Privacy Policy

How NeoVid Converter for iPhone and iPad handles your information.

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Application: NeoVid Converter for iPhone and iPad (com.neovid.converter) · Developer: NetSoftTouch (Nguyen Minh Thuan)

This policy covers NeoVid Converter on iPhone and iPad, distributed on the Apple App Store. The Android app works differently and has its own privacy policy. A short summary in Vietnamese is at the end.

Summary: NeoVid Converter collects no data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting service and no user accounts. Your videos and audio are converted on your device by the FFmpeg libraries built into the app and are never uploaded. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send us an email, and purchases go through Apple.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your media: the videos, songs and recordings you open, and the files the app makes from them, are processed and stored only on your device. Their names and folder paths are never sent anywhere.
  • Accounts: the app has no registration or login and never asks for your name, email address or password.
  • Usage, advertising and location data: the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not read your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), does not track you across apps or websites, never asks for your location, and does not send usage statistics to us. It never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track you.

2. Information Kept on Your Device

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them:

  • Your library: converted files, and a list of them with their name, size, length and the tool that made them.
  • Settings: your choices, such as default formats, theme and language.
  • Free plan counters: how many conversions you have left today, and how many free tries of each Pro option you have used (see the Terms of Use). They are stored in the device Keychain, tied to no account and used for nothing else. The Keychain can keep them after the app is deleted, so reinstalling does not restore used conversions or tries.
  • Crash and freeze reports: see Section 3.C.

3. Services the App Relies On

A. In-App Purchases (the App Store)

If you buy NeoVid Pro, the purchase is made through the App Store: Apple processes the payment under its own terms, and the app receives only which Pro product you own and the details of that purchase (such as its date and transaction number). With each purchase the app gives Apple a random identifier it created itself, so that a purchase can be matched to this installation; it contains nothing about you. NetSoftTouch does not receive, store or have access to your card, bank or Apple Account details.

B. Emails You Choose to Send

If you write to us, or use Settings → Feedback, your Mail app opens with a draft that includes your device model, iOS version, app version, plan and, if you bought Pro, its App Store transaction number, so we can help with a purchase question. If a conversion fails, the bug button in its conversion log adds the technical details of that conversion, with file names, folder paths and web addresses removed. You see the whole email and decide whether to send it. We use what you send only to answer you and fix the problem.

C. Crash Reports You Choose to Send

When the app crashes or freezes, iOS may give it a technical report (through Apple’s MetricKit). NeoVid Converter keeps the most recent reports on your device. They contain stack traces and device details such as the model and iOS version, never your media or file names. Nothing is sent automatically: if you choose Settings → Send diagnostic report, your Mail app opens with the reports attached and you decide whether to send the email. Reports are deleted from your device once sent.

Separately, if you have turned on Share iPhone Analytics / Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash logs with developers through Apple’s own systems. That is controlled by Apple’s settings and privacy policy, not by the app.

4. Network Use

Converting, cutting, merging, compressing and playing files never use the network, and the app works offline. It connects to the internet only for:

  • Purchases: showing prices, buying, restoring and checking NeoVid Pro, through the App Store.
  • Links you open: this privacy policy, the terms of use, the open-source source code and store pages open in Safari or the App Store app.

Information shown to other parts of your device

These stay on your device and your own accessories, and are not sent to us:

  • Files app: converted files are kept in the app’s folder, which you can open in the Files app (On My iPhone → NeoVid Converter) or through Finder on a connected Mac.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the app tells you when a conversion finishes or fails. The notification is created on the device; no push server is involved.
  • Playback: while you play a file in the app, its name is shown on the Lock Screen and in Control Center, and to accessories such as AirPods, the same as for any audio app.
  • Sharing into the app: when you share files to NeoVid Converter from another app, its share extension copies only those files into the app’s storage on your device.

5. Device Permissions and Why They Are Needed

  • Photos and Files: videos and audio are chosen with Apple’s system pickers. The app receives only the items you pick, so iOS does not ask for a permission.
  • Music library: asked for only when you first pick a song from your music library. The app reads only the songs you choose; protected Apple Music tracks cannot be used.
  • Add to Photos: asked for only when you first save a video or GIF to Photos. The app can add items but cannot see your photo library.
  • Notifications: optional, for finished conversions (Section 4).
  • Camera and microphone: never used. The media framework built into the app includes capture code that the app never turns on, and Apple requires a description of it in the app; iOS never asks you for these permissions.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

NeoVid Converter is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us at nguyenminhthuanvnn@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your files, library and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them, so we hold no copy to delete:

  • You can delete any converted file from the app’s library at any time.
  • Deleting NeoVid Converter from your iPhone or iPad removes everything it stored, including your converted files, library, settings and kept crash reports. Move any file you want to keep to Photos or to another folder in the Files app first. The free plan counters in the Keychain (Section 2) may stay on the device.
  • Device backups: like most apps, the app’s storage may be included when you back up your device to iCloud or a computer. Those backups are made by Apple and you, are covered by Apple’s privacy policy, and are never sent to us.
  • Emails you send us are kept in our mailbox only as long as needed to answer you and fix the problem. Ask us at any time to delete them.

How Data Is Protected

  • The only connections the app makes (Section 4) go through Apple over encrypted connections.
  • Your files are kept in the app’s private storage, which iOS prevents other apps from reading.
  • We never receive your files, so there is no copy of them on any server of ours to be exposed.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. NeoVid Converter does not collect, sell or share personal information: your media and settings exist only on your device, where you can view and delete them directly. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to access or delete that correspondence. You are welcome to contact us with any privacy question.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
