Float Video Player

A floating-window video and audio player for Android. Watch in a movable, resizable window on top of whatever else you are doing, or keep listening with the screen off.

By NetSoftTouch · Package com.floatvideo.free

What it does

Floating windowMovable, resizable, over any other app
Multi-windowSeveral videos at once, or one at a time
Background audioKeep playing with the screen off
Music libraryBrowse and play your device audio
File browserInternal storage, SD cards and USB drives
PlaylistsBuild and manage your own
Equalizer & sleep timerPresets, and stop playback on a timer
Auto-resumePicks up where you left off

Plays MP4, MKV, FLV, AVI, MOV, WMV, TS, M2TS, WebM and more, from local storage, your file manager, or the Share menu of another app. Playback is controlled from the notification panel, the lock screen, and headset or Bluetooth media buttons.

Ads and privacy

  • The app is free and supported by ads (Google AdMob). There is no paid tier.
  • Users in the EEA and the UK are asked for consent before any personalised ad is shown, and can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the app.
  • Anonymous usage and crash reporting can be turned off from Settings → Usage & crash reporting.
  • Full details are in the Float Video Player privacy policy.
